TEXAS UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES

All rules apply to all new and renewal policies, unless otherwise indicated. Agents may bind
risks meeting the criteria for eligibility outlined in this Underwriting manual.

A. General

a. Residence must be used for private residential purposes only.

b. Named insured must be an individual(s).

c. Dwellings must be insured to 98-105% of replacement cost verified by the
company’s estimator tool (HO3).

d. Agent binding authority up to Coverage A $1,250,000. Higher values of up to
$5,000,000 may be submitted for underwriter review and possible consent to
rate acceptance.

e. Dwellings must be in good condition, free of maintenance concerns and free of
existing damage.

f. Any applicant who has ever been convicted of any degree of arson is ineligible.

g. Any applicant who has been a first party in a personal lawsuit against an auto
and/or homeowners insurance company is ineligible. Risks where the applicant
prevailed in or settled the lawsuit will be eligible.

h. One or two -family, owner-occupied dwellings only.

i. The Company reserves the right to decline or cancel any risk with a trampoline as
deemed necessary by Underwriting. Coverage is not provided for any liability
involving a trampoline.

j. The Company reserves the right to decline or cancel any risk with a pool slide
and/or pool diving board as deemed necessary by Underwriting. Coverage is not
provided for any liability involving swimming pool diving boards and/or pool
Slides.

k. Dwellings located in a Special Flood Hazard Area (Zone A or V) must be covered
by a Federal Flood Insurance policy or customer must sign a Flood Rejection
Form. Copy of the Flood Policy dec page must be retained by the agent. Copy of
the signed Flood Rejection Form must be submitted to the carrier.

|. Post bind inspections will be performed at New Business as deemed necessary.
The Company reserves the right to inspect any risk as deemed necessary by
Underwriting.

B. Binding Authority

a. Risks may be bound up to sixty (60) days prior to the policy effective date. A risk
is not considered bound until a policy is issued in our rating system. This
timeframe may be reduced when rate filing effective dates are approaching.

b. Trailing Document Requirements:

i. Pre-bind documents must be attached prior to binding. All required post-
bind documents must be submitted within seven (7) days of the policy
effective date. Attach the documents to the policy by clicking on the
“Attachments” tab within the rating/policy system.

ii. Pre-bind: Appraisals as outlined in the Coverage section of this manual,
IBHS certified Hurricane Loss Mitigation Survey form or Fortified for Safer
Homes certification, Windstorm and Hail Rejection form, Flood Rejection
form, Contents Rejection form, if any are applicable.

iii. Post-bind: Proof of renovation updates, appraisals as outlined in Coverage
section of this manual, proof of sprinkler system and alarm systems,
(fire/burglary) if any are applicable.

iv. Retainin office: Application signed and dated by insured and agent.
Companion Auto/Umbrella declaration pages, if applicable.

c. Inthe event of a hurricane or tropical storm, agents must refer to the Company’s
policy administration system website for binding authority restrictions. The
restriction will also apply to endorsement requests for increased coverage or
reduction in any deductible amount. The duration of time and the geographic
scope of this restriction will be determined by the Company at the time of the
event.

C. General Underwriting

A. Minimum deductible:

a. Aminimum of 1% windstorm or hail deductible is required in all counties that are
open for binding.

b. Aminimum of 2% hurricane deductible is required in all counties that are open
for binding.

B. Roof Condition and Type:

a. Flat roofs, rock or tar roofs, gravel roofs or roofs with asbestos shingles, wood,
rolled roofs, sheet tin, aluminum (including galvanized), and roofs with more
than one layer of outer shingles or covering are ineligible for coverage.

b. Metal, steel, or aluminum roofs must be written with the Exclusion of Cosmetic
Damage to Roof and Siding Coverings Caused by Windstorm or Hail (ECD).

c. Based on roof age and material, The Company may inspect roofs to determine
condition and eligibility. The Company reserves the right to decline coverage or
modify loss provisions for roof surfacing to actual cash value (using form ACVR)
on arisk with roofs not in acceptable condition as determined by Underwriting

Please note the Company’s underwriting recommendations for the application of the Actual

Asphalt Shingle / 3 Tab 6 years or older
Architectural or Composition Shingle 10 years or older
Metal, Slate, Tile, Concrete 15 years or older

C. Loss History:
a. These risks will be declined at New Business or cancelled within the underwriting
review period or are not eligible for renewal if the policy is in effect three (3)
years or less:
i. Risks with more than one non-weather loss.

ii. Risks with any non-weather loss that occurs within the underwriting review
period, excluding losses protected under Texas Insurance Code §544.353
(appliance-related water damage claims that have been properly remediated
and certified) or Texas Insurance Code §544.303 (mold-related claims where
remediation has been completed and evidenced as required).

iii. Risks with open claims.

iv. Any liability and/or fire claims.

b. Prior mold-related claims will not affect a consumer's eligibility for coverage or any
underwriting decision, provided all of the following conditions are met:

i. The applicant has property eligible for coverage under a residential property
insurance policy;
ii. The property has had prior mold damage;
iii. The mold damage has been remediated; and
iv. The remediation is evidenced by either:
e A Certificate of Mold Remediation issued under Texas Occupations

Code §1958.154, establishing with reasonable certainty that the
underlying cause of the mold has been remediated; or

e Aninspection by an independent assessor or adjuster who
determined, based on the inspection, that the property no longer
contains evidence of mold damage.

=====PAGE 5=====
c. Wewill not consider a prior appliance-related water damage claim as a basis for any
underwriting decision — including the decision to issue, renew, cancel, or rate a policy
— provided the requirements of Texas Insurance Code §544.353(c) or §544.353(d), as
applicable, are satisfied. This prohibition applies to a maximum of two (2) such claims
per insured or per covered property within any three-year period. A third or
subsequent appliance-related claim within that same three-year period is not entitled
to protected status and may be considered in our underwriting decisions pursuant to
Texas Insurance Code §544.353(e).

d. Our decision to refuse to renew a policy is subject to the following conditions:

i. We will not consider the following claim types when determining whether to
refuse renewal:

e Claims resulting from a loss caused by natural causes;

e Claims that were filed but are not paid or payable under the policy;
or

e Appliance-related water damage claims we are prohibited from
using under Texas Insurance Code §544.353.

ii. We may refuse to renew a policy only if the insured has filed three (3) or more
claims under the policy within any three-year period, excluding the claim types
identified in section (i) above.

D. Older Homes/Age of Major Systems:

a. Dwellings 40 years and older will be written with the Limited Water Damage
Coverage endorsement (LWD) at a $10,000 limit. Higher sublimits may be
available, up to a maximum of 10% of Coverage A or $50,000, whichever is less.
Requests for removal of LWD will be determined based on, but not limited to, the
overall condition of the plumbing system, the condition of the home, and the age
and location of the risk. Proof of significant upgrades and renovations to current
plumbing standards will be required, and approval is subject to Underwriter
discretion.

b. Dwellings with fuses, cloth, double tap, knob and tube, or aluminum wiring are
not eligible.

c. Dwellings with Federal Pacific, Stab-lok, GTE Sylvania, Zinsco, Challenger
electrical panels are ineligible.

=====PAGE 6=====
d. Dwellings must have electrical and plumbing systems that are in good condition
according to current standards. These systems will be reviewed for condition if
the property is 40+ years old unless the system in question has been updated in
the past 20 years.

e. Dwellings with evaporative cooling systems and/or without central air/heat are
not eligible.

f. HVAC units (condenser and air handler) older than 20 years will be reviewed for
eligibility. HVAC units (including the condenser and air handler) found to be in
poor condition will be deemed unacceptable.

g. Dwellings with Polybutylene, cast-iron and/or galvanized plumbing are not
eligible.

h. Dwellings with water heaters found to be in poor condition are unacceptable.
Water heaters will be reviewed for condition based upon age, as follows:

i. Ten (10) years or older if located inside the dwelling.
ii. Fifteen (15) years or older if located outside the dwelling/living area.
E. Ineligible Risks:

a. Vacant dwellings

b. Dwellings under construction, including dwellings undergoing construction/major
remodeling may be referred to underwriting for consideration with the Dwelling
in the Course of Construction endorsement.

c. Mobile homes, modular homes, manufactured homes and boathouses.

d. Dwellings of unusual construction. This includes, but is not limited to
cantilevered construction, log construction, barndominiums, or homes which
require knowledge of special reconstruction techniques.

e. Dwellings built on stilts and/or pylons before 2021.

f. Dwellings with aluminum, wood shake, or asbestos siding, or any mix thereof.

g. Dwellings with any components containing asbestos, including but not limited to
siding, roofing, insulation or flooring material.

h. Dwellings with flat roofs, or roofs with asbestos shingles, wood shake shingles,
rolled roofs, sheet tin, aluminum (including galvanized), tar and gravel roof, built-
up roofs, and roofs with more than one layer of outer shingles or covering.

i. Dwellings with EIFS construction (Exterior Insulation Finishing System) or Dryvit
siding (unless used as trim or comprising only a minimal portion of the total
siding-will be reviewed by Underwriting).

j. Dwellings located in Protection Class 9 or 10.

k. Dwellings located partially over/over water.

|. Dwellings with in-ground/buried oil tanks.

m. Dwellings with existing damage.

n. Dwellings and/or detached structures that fail to exhibit reasonable maintenance
standards or pride of ownership are ineligible for coverage. Any property
displaying deferred maintenance, structural deterioration, or conditions that

o. Unfenced pools or pools with less than a five (5) foot high fence, or pools without

p. Hazardous animal exposure (e.g., animals generally found in the wild and/or ina

q. Risks with more than two (2) horses, cows, pigs, goats, sheep, etc.

r. Any animal with prior history of biting and/or attacking persons and/or property
and/or other animals.

Causasian, Mountain Dogs, Chow Chows, Doberman Pinschers, German
Shepherds, Great Danes, Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Staffordshire Terriers, and Wolf
Hybrids.

t. Notwithstanding sections (r) and (s) above, the restrictions set forth in this
section do not apply to a trained service animal as defined under the
Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), 42 U.S.C. §12102, that is individually
trained to perform specific tasks for the benefit of a person with a disability.

To qualify for this exception, the insured may be required to provide
documentation confirming the animal's status as a trained service animal,

i. Verification of the specific task or tasks the animal has been trained to
perform; and
ii. Confirmation that the animal is currently being used by a person with a

Coverage under Animal Liability Endorsement (CSH TX AL) for Service Animals
is limited to a maximum $50,000.

u. Business on premises.

v. Dwellings with bars on windows, unless all are equipped with quick release.

w. Dwellings with exceptionally steep stairs. Stairs and porches of significant height
without railings (subject to Underwriting review).

F. Windstorm Exclusions:

a. The perils of Windstorm, Hail, and Hurricane may be excluded using form
Windstorm Hail Exclusion (WHHE), so long as the wind perils are covered by a
separate in force policy from the Texas Wind Insurance Association (TWIA) which
maintains the same Coverage A limits.

G. Isolated Properties:

a. Risks located on islands not accessible by major roadway are ineligible. This
includes islands accessible only by ferry.

b. Risks not visible from either the roadway or a neighboring property.

a. Inthe event of impending severe weather conditions, hurricane or tropical
storms or civil unrest, agents must refer to The Company’s policy administration
system website for binding authority restrictions. The restriction will also apply to
endorsement requests for increased coverage or reduction in any deductible
amount. The duration of time and the geographic scope of this restriction will

be determined by the Company at the time of event.

d. HO4/HO6-Total schedules over 20% of Coverage C, or $50,000 (whichever is lower) are

e. Scheduled personal property requests where the named insured has had/does have any
